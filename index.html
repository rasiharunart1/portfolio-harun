About

Where hardware meets intelligence.

I'm Harun Ar Rasyid, an Informatics Engineering student at Telkom University Purwokerto with a focused interest in systems that bridge the physical and digital worlds. My work spans embedded electronics, computer vision, and full-stack web development.

My foundation was laid in vocational school studying Audio Video Electronics — giving me hands-on hardware literacy that most software engineers lack. Since then I've grown into designing complete IoT pipelines, from writing firmware on ESP32 to training YOLO detection models and deploying the web dashboards that make the data meaningful to real users.

I believe great engineering is quiet. The best systems are the ones users never have to think about. That philosophy drives every project — whether it's a serverless smart home, a fish-counting vision system, or real-time traffic monitoring infrastructure for city government.

Hardware-first mindset

Grounded in electronics from vocational training, I understand hardware constraints that pure software engineers overlook.

Vision & Intelligence

Experienced deploying YOLO models on edge devices for real-world detection and counting tasks at production scale.

Full-stack IoT

From ESP32 firmware to Laravel backends and Next.js dashboards — I ship the entire stack, not just one layer.

Based in Banyumas, Indonesia

Working with local government and private clients across Central Java. Open to remote collaboration globally.
